Combat quick reference

On your turnMove up to your Speed and take one action. You can also interact with one object or feature as allowed.
Common actionsAttack · Dash · Disengage · Dodge · Help · Hide · Ready · Search · Study · Utilize · Magic
Bonus actionOnly available when a feature, spell or other rule gives you one.
ReactionOne reaction per round, taken in response to a trigger. It refreshes at the start of your next turn.
Opportunity attackUsually triggered when a creature you can see leaves your reach using its movement, action or reaction.
Critical hitOn a natural 20 on an attack roll, roll the attack's damage dice twice and add modifiers normally.

Spellcasting quick reference

Spell attackProficiency bonus + spellcasting ability modifier
Spell save DC8 + proficiency bonus + spellcasting ability modifier
ConcentrationYou can concentrate on only one spell at a time. Damage may force a Constitution saving throw.
RitualA spell with the Ritual tag can be cast as a ritual if you have it prepared, adding 10 minutes to its casting time.
ComponentsVerbal (V), Somatic (S), Material (M). A focus can replace material components unless they have a listed cost or are consumed.
